What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, to remember your preferences between visits, and to provide information to site owners about how their site is being used.

We also use related technologies such as pixels, local storage and software development kits. For simplicity, this policy refers to all of them as “cookies”.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to keep the website working correctly, to remember choices you make, to measure how the site performs, and — where you permit it — to measure the effectiveness of our advertising. We do not use cookies to collect sensitive personal information, and we do not sell cookie data.

First-Party and Third-Party Cookies

First-party cookies are set by this website directly. They are used for essential functionality and for basic measurement of site usage.

Third-party cookies are set by external services we use. These may include website analytics providers, advertising platforms such as Meta and Google, embedded content such as maps or video players, and social media sharing tools. These providers set cookies according to their own policies, which we encourage you to review.

How Long Cookies Last

  • Session cookies are temporary and are deleted automatically when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies remain on your device for a set period, which may range from a few days to around two years depending on their purpose, or until you delete them.

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can control and delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to view the cookies stored, delete them individually or entirely, block cookies from particular sites, and block third-party cookies altogether.

Instructions are available in the help section of each browser, including Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Safari, Microsoft Edge and Opera. On mobile devices, similar controls are found in the browser or operating system settings.

Please note. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may cause parts of the website, such as forms, to stop working as intended. Blocking analytics or marketing cookies will not affect your ability to browse the site.

Opting Out of Advertising Cookies

In addition to browser controls, you can manage advertising preferences directly with the platforms involved. Meta, Google and most major advertising networks provide their own ad settings pages where you can limit personalised advertising. Industry opt-out tools operated by digital advertising associations also allow you to opt out of several networks at once.

Do Not Track

Some browsers transmit a “Do Not Track” signal. There is currently no common industry standard for how such signals should be interpreted. Our website does not respond to Do Not Track signals at present, but you can achieve a similar result using the browser and platform controls described above.

Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as our website and the technologies we use change. The revision date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated. Please review it periodically.

If you have questions about our use of cookies, contact us at info@leaderdigitalmedia.com. For broader information on how we handle personal data, see our Privacy Policy.
