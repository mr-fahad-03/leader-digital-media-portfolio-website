Frequently asked questions

The questions we are asked most often, answered plainly. If yours is not here, send it to us directly and we will answer it.

We provide social media marketing, search engine optimisation, web design and development, paid media buying, branding and identity, content and video production, e-commerce solutions, and analytics consulting. Services can be engaged individually as a project or combined into a single monthly retainer.

Fees depend entirely on scope, so we do not publish fixed package prices. After an initial conversation we send a written proposal setting out deliverables, timelines and a fixed monthly fee or project price. There are no percentage-of-ad-spend charges and no fees that appear later without being agreed first.

Yes. Our office is in Karachi and we meet local clients in person, but a large share of our work is handled remotely for clients elsewhere in Pakistan and overseas. Reviews run over video call and all documentation is shared digitally.

It depends on the channel. Paid advertising can produce measurable results within the first two to four weeks once tracking is in place. Search engine optimisation is slower, typically showing meaningful movement from month three onward and compounding after that. Brand and content work shows up gradually in the quality of enquiries rather than in an immediate spike. We will give you a realistic timeline in the proposal rather than an optimistic one.

Retainers carry a minimum initial term of three months, because shorter periods do not give the work enough time to be judged fairly. After that initial term the arrangement continues month to month and can be ended with thirty days' written notice. Project work has no ongoing commitment at all.

You do. Ad accounts, analytics properties, domains, hosting and social profiles are registered in your company's name, with us added as a user. Final creative deliverables transfer to you on full payment. If we stop working together, you keep everything and we remove our access.

You receive a written monthly report covering what was delivered, what it cost, the results against the targets agreed at the start, and the plan for the following month. Most clients also get a live dashboard they can open at any time. We hold a review call each month to go through it properly.

Access to your existing accounts, your brand assets if you have them, a clear statement of your commercial objectives, and one person on your side empowered to approve work. The less approval sits in a committee, the faster everything moves.

Yes. Photography, video, motion graphics, design and copywriting are produced by our own team. This keeps campaigns on schedule and keeps quality consistent, rather than depending on a chain of freelancers.

Regularly. We start with an audit of what exists, document what is working, and plan a transition that does not interrupt live campaigns. We will give you a frank assessment of the current setup, including the parts that are fine and should be left alone.

We invoice in Pakistani Rupees and accept bank transfer to our company account. International clients can be invoiced in other currencies by prior arrangement. Retainers are invoiced in advance for each month; project work is billed against agreed milestones.

We reply to new enquiries within one business day. Existing clients reach their account lead directly by phone, email or WhatsApp during business hours, which are Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 19:00 Pakistan Standard Time.

Still have a question? Email us at info@leaderdigitalmedia.com or use the contact form. We reply within one business day.
