Introduction

Leader Digital Media (SMC-Private) Limited (“Leader Digital Media”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is a company incorporated in Pakistan with its registered office at Unit No. 10, Ramp Area, Shaikh Sultan Trust, Karachi 74200, Pakistan. We are committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who visits our website, enquires about our services or becomes a client.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and store it, who we share it with, and the choices available to you. It applies to our website and to the services we provide in the course of our business.

By using our website or submitting an enquiry, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use our website or submit information to us.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information in three ways: information you give us directly, information collected automatically when you use our website, and information we receive from third parties.

Information you provide to us

  • Contact details — your name, company name, email address, telephone number and postal address.
  • Enquiry details — the service you are interested in, your indicative budget, and anything you tell us about your project in a form, email, call or meeting.
  • Client records — if you become a client, the contractual, billing and account information necessary to deliver and invoice for our services.
  • Recruitment information — if you apply to work with us, your CV and the details in your application.
  • Correspondence — records of messages exchanged with us, including by email and messaging applications.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data — your IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, screen size and language settings.
  • Usage data — pages viewed, time spent on pages, links clicked, referring website and the path you take through our site.
  • Cookie data — information stored by cookies and similar technologies, described in our Cookie Policy.

Information from third parties

  • Analytics and advertising platforms that report on how visitors reach and use our website.
  • Publicly available business sources, such as company websites and professional networks, used in the ordinary course of business development.
  • Referrals from existing clients or partners who pass on your contact details with your knowledge.

How We Use Your Information

We use personal information only for purposes connected with running our business and providing our services. Specifically, we use it to:

  • respond to enquiries and prepare proposals and quotations;
  • deliver the services agreed in a contract, including campaign management and reporting;
  • issue invoices, process payments and keep accounting records;
  • communicate with you about your project, account or any changes to our services;
  • operate, maintain, secure and improve our website;
  • analyse website traffic in order to understand what visitors find useful;
  • send marketing communications where you have asked to receive them or are an existing client, subject always to your right to opt out;
  • consider applications for employment or contracting work;
  • comply with our legal, tax, regulatory and accounting obligations in Pakistan; and
  • establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
We do not sell your personal information. We do not trade, rent or sell personal data to third parties for their own marketing purposes under any circumstances.

Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal information where we have a proper basis to do so. Depending on the circumstances, that basis will be:

  • Performance of a contract — where processing is necessary to deliver services you have engaged us for, or to take steps at your request before entering a contract.
  • Legitimate interests — where processing is necessary for the ordinary operation of our business, such as responding to enquiries, securing our systems and improving our services, provided your rights do not override those interests.
  • Consent — where you have given clear permission, for example to receive marketing emails or to allow non-essential cookies. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation — where we are required to process information to comply with the law, including tax and corporate record-keeping requirements.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to function correctly, to remember your preferences and to help us understand how the site is used. Some cookies are strictly necessary for the website to operate; others are optional.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, and blocking non-essential cookies will not prevent you from using the site. Full details of the categories of cookies we use and how to manage them are set out in our Cookie Policy.

How We Share Information

We share personal information only where it is necessary, and only with parties who are obliged to protect it. The categories of recipient are:

  • Service providers — hosting providers, email services, analytics platforms, advertising platforms, accounting software and payment processors that support our operations.
  • Professional advisers — lawyers, auditors, accountants and insurers, where necessary and under a duty of confidentiality.
  • Authorities — government bodies, regulators, courts or law enforcement, where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect our legal rights.
  • Business transfers — a buyer or successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition or reorganisation of our business, subject to equivalent protection.

Where we engage a service provider, we require that they process personal information only on our instructions and apply appropriate security measures.

International Transfers

Some of the service providers we rely on, including hosting, email and analytics providers, operate servers outside Pakistan. This means your personal information may be stored or processed in other countries, which may have different data protection standards.

Where information is transferred internationally, we take reasonable steps to ensure it remains protected to a standard consistent with this policy, including by selecting reputable providers with recognised security and privacy commitments.

Data Security

We maintain technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure. These include:

  • encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) across our website;
  • access controls restricting data to staff who need it for their role;
  • strong authentication on business systems and client accounts;
  • regular software updates and security patching;
  • routine backups of business-critical data; and
  • confidentiality obligations binding on all staff and contractors.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we take security seriously, we cannot guarantee absolute security, and any transmission of information to us is at your own risk.

Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as there is a reason to keep it. In practice:

Type of informationRetention period
Enquiries that do not become clientsUp to 24 months from last contact
Client contracts and project recordsDuration of the engagement plus 7 years
Invoices and accounting recordsAs required by Pakistani tax and corporate law
Marketing subscriber detailsUntil you unsubscribe or ask to be removed
Recruitment applications12 months, unless you ask us to keep them longer
Website analytics dataTypically up to 26 months, as configured in the platform

When information is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymise it so that it can no longer be linked to you.

Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you and receive a copy of it.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete information where there is no continuing reason for us to hold it.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing, including direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, without affecting processing already carried out.
  • Receive a copy of information you provided to us in a commonly used electronic format.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@leaderdigitalmedia.com. We will respond within thirty days. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and in limited cases we may be unable to comply fully — for example where we are legally required to retain certain records. If so, we will explain why.

Marketing Communications

We send marketing emails only to people who have asked for them or who are existing clients with a relevant interest in what we are sending. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also email info@leaderdigitalmedia.com at any time to be removed.

Opting out of marketing does not stop service-related messages such as invoices, campaign reports or responses to your enquiries, which are necessary for the operation of your account.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are directed at businesses and are not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of eighteen. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Third-Party Websites

Our website contains links to external sites, including social media platforms and the websites of clients and partners. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of any website you visit through a link from ours.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices, our services or the law. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Where changes are significant, we will take reasonable steps to notify clients directly. Continued use of our website after a change takes effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, or wish to make a request regarding your personal information, please contact us:
