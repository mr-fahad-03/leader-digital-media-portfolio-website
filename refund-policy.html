Purpose of This Policy

This Refund and Cancellation Policy explains how deposits, cancellations, refunds and terminations are handled by Leader Digital Media (SMC-Private) Limited. It applies to all services unless a signed service agreement expressly provides otherwise, in which case that agreement prevails.

Our aim is to be straightforward about this. If an engagement is not working, we would rather resolve it properly than hold a client to a contract they no longer want.

Nature of Our Services

We provide professional services rather than physical goods. Time, creative work, media management and third-party costs are committed as work proceeds and generally cannot be recovered once incurred. Refunds are therefore assessed against work already performed and costs already committed, not against the total contract value.

Advance Payments and Deposits

Project engagements usually require an advance payment before work begins. This secures your place in our production schedule and covers initial strategy and discovery work.

  • Advance payments are non-refundable once discovery or production work has commenced.
  • If you cancel in writing before any work has begun, the advance is refundable in full less any third-party costs already committed on your behalf.
  • Where a project is placed on hold at the Client's request, the advance remains credited to the engagement for six months.

Monthly Retainers

Retainers are invoiced in advance for each service month.

  • Retainer fees for a month that has already begun are not refundable, as the team and schedule for that month are committed.
  • Retainers carry a minimum initial term stated in the Proposal, normally three months.
  • After the initial term, either party may terminate with thirty days' written notice. Services continue, and fees remain payable, through the notice period.
  • If notice is given before an upcoming month is invoiced, no further fees are charged beyond the notice period.

Project Milestones

Project work is billed against agreed milestones. If a project is cancelled part-way:

  • fees for completed and in-progress milestones remain payable in full;
  • fees for milestones not yet started are not charged, and any amount already paid for them is refunded;
  • deliverables completed up to the cancellation date are handed over once all outstanding amounts are settled.

Advertising Spend

Media budgets are distinct from our management fees.

  • Where the Client funds advertising directly on their own payment method, we never hold those funds and no refund arises from us.
  • Where the Client has paid media budget to us in advance, any unspent balance is refunded in full within fifteen business days of the campaign ending or the engagement terminating.
  • Amounts already spent with a platform cannot be recovered. Management fees relating to spend already delivered are not refundable.

Revisions and Dissatisfaction

Each Proposal specifies the number of revision rounds included. If you are not satisfied with a deliverable, raise it with your account lead and we will work through the included revisions to resolve it.

Dissatisfaction with creative direction after the included revisions have been used does not by itself create an entitlement to a refund. Additional revisions beyond the agreed rounds will be quoted separately before any further work is carried out.

Circumstances Where Refunds Are Not Available

  • Work that has been completed and delivered in accordance with the agreed scope.
  • Campaign performance that falls short of expectations, where the services were delivered as specified. Results depend on factors outside our control, as set out in our Terms and Conditions.
  • Delays or failures caused by the Client not supplying assets, access, information or approvals.
  • Advertising spend already delivered by a third-party platform.
  • Third-party costs such as domains, hosting, licences and stock media, once purchased.
  • Termination by us for non-payment or material breach by the Client.

How to Request a Refund or Cancel

All cancellation and refund requests must be made in writing to info@leaderdigitalmedia.com and should include:

  • the Client and project name;
  • the invoice number or numbers concerned;
  • the reason for the request; and
  • any supporting detail you wish us to consider.

We acknowledge requests within two business days and provide a written decision within ten business days. Approved refunds are processed within fifteen business days to the original payment account. Bank charges or currency conversion costs on international transfers are borne by the Client.

Disputes

If you disagree with a decision, write to us setting out your position and we will review it at senior level. We would always prefer to resolve a dispute through discussion. Where that is not possible, the dispute resolution and jurisdiction provisions in our Terms and Conditions apply.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version in effect at the time your Proposal was accepted governs that engagement. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
